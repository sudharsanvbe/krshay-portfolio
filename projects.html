Executive Summary

Measuring recovery through movement.

An IMU-based embedded system using an ESP32 to quantify ACL rehabilitation recovery — objective, data-driven tracking as an alternative to subjective clinical assessment.

02
The Problem

From subjective assessment to data.

Traditional ACL recovery tracking relies on periodic clinical evaluations, lacking the granularity to capture daily movement deviations.
